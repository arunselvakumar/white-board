## Decisions

The sixteen open questions of `modules/07` are answered in [ADR CM-0017](../../adr/CM-0017-finance-product-decisions.md).

**The owner settled four before the build:**

- **One ledger:** every payment names its account and posts there with the party's entry.
- **TDS 194C computed**, with a TDS ledger and override with a reason; 194I, 194J, 194Q and Form 26Q / 16A in M11.
- **Only approved entries move balances;** corrections are reversals.
- **One payment settles several invoices,** the rest is an advance.

Every rule the build settled is in `modules/07` → "Decisions for the build", by ticket. These are the ones most worth a look:

- **A Transaction never moves a party's balance.** Its Paid To is a label; paying a party against its dues is a party payment. This keeps the same rupee from being counted twice.
- **Party payments, wage and salary payments have no approval** (none in legacy); they post when recorded and Cancel reverses them. Transactions, transfers, vouchers, Other Expenses and settlements do need approval.
- **Approve and reject check only the Financial Closing Date**; create, edit and delete also check the back-dated day limits.
- **Balances may go negative** (a site engineer spends first); the screen shows them in red.
- **Finance files never join the Project Gallery.**
- **A GRN's invoice takes its number, date and amount from the GRN**; change them on the GRN.
- **Overdue needs a due date;** an invoice without one ages as Not due.
- **Store-located supplier invoices and payments are the Owner's alone** (a Store has no Project to scope the menu).

## Stubs and gaps left for later

- **Wage payments recorded before M7 have no account** and are not on any account's ledger.
- **HRMS has no "un-pay"**, so a Paid slip's entry is never reversed.
- **Not in M7** (CM-0017 §17): RA bills, retention and mobilisation advances; GST ITC / RCM / e-invoice; 194I on equipment hire (`EquipmentHireAccrued` still has no listener), 194J, 194Q, challans, Form 26Q / 16A; UPI links, the RERA account, Tally / Zoho sync, bank-statement import, the vendor portal; equipment maintenance cost as an expense; multi-level approval and amount thresholds.
- **Notifications** for approvals and Export Receipts are M9's (the ZIP downloads in the request, up to 500 files).
- **Store screens:** Store-located supplier invoices work in the API and Central Payment, but there is no Store page for them.
- **Dev-server only:** `next dev` writes untracked `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` into `apps/construction-management`; delete them, never commit them.

## Open questions for the owner

1. Are the CM-0017 defaults right? In particular:
   - a Transaction's Paid To being a label that never moves a party balance;
   - party payments posting without approval while Transactions need it;
   - negative account balances allowed;
   - finance files kept out of the Gallery.
2. TDS is deducted **at payment**. 194C strictly applies at credit or payment, whichever is earlier. Should a contractor **bill** above the thresholds book TDS when it is entered (it matters when bills are paid much later)?
3. Should a member be able to raise a Company-level Transaction (no Project), or only the Owner?
4. Wage payments before M7 have no account. Do you want a one-off screen to assign them to an account (it would post them to the ledger on their old dates)?
5. Export Receipts is capped at 500 files per download until M9's job queue. Is that enough for a month of a busy site?
