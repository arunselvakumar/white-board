## Stubs and gaps left for later

- **No money:** unit pricing, payment plans, demand letters, collections, GST on sales, the RERA registration gate and the allottee portal are M11 / M12 (CM-0022 §12).
- **Reminders** are a daily notice, not at the follow-up's minute (Vercel Hobby has daily crons only).
- **No Company-wide sales view**, no moving an inquiry to another Project, no lead consent records, no single inquiry or booking print.
- **Web push** for the sales notifications was not tried in a real browser (same as M9).

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Duplicates:** warn only within a Project (today), or across the Company's Projects too?
2. **View all:** should Bookings get a View all flag, so sales executives see only their own bookings?
3. **Holds:** should a hold need a Hold until (today it is optional and can last forever)?
4. **Conversion rate:** Converted ÷ Total (legacy, today) or Converted ÷ (Converted + Lost)?
5. **Unit import:** should it also create units missing from the Wing (today it only updates existing ones)?
6. **Reopen:** should reopening a Converted inquiry offer to cancel its booking in the same step?
7. **Notifications:** should Lead Owners also get `inquiry.follow_ups_due`, or only assignees?
