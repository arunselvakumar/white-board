## Decisions

The seventeen open questions of `modules/06` are answered in [ADR CM-0015](../../adr/CM-0015-procurement-inventory-product-decisions.md). The owner settled four before the build: **stock never goes below zero**, **transfers and Delivery Notes leave at dispatch and arrive on delivery**, **GST is split by state with ITC / RCM / TDS left for M11**, and **one PR for the milestone**. Every rule the build settled is in `modules/06` and `modules/02` → "Decisions for the build", by ticket. The ones most worth a look:

- **The ledger is append-only.** Corrections are reversals plus new entries; legacy's editable history becomes Adjust stock. A back-dated movement is refused if it would empty stock that a later movement already used.
- **Approval and fulfilment are separate statuses** on every document (PR: approval + order status; PO: approval + Ordered + receipt status + Close; MR: requested / partially delivered / delivered / closed).
- **A store refuses** what it cannot supply by **closing** the rest of a Material Request (there is no reject on MR or DN).
- **Purchase Orders have no Financial flag**, so PO amounts show to everyone who can read POs; GRN amounts follow Material Received's Financial.
- **Anyone who can read a document can comment on it**; attaching or removing files needs Create or Update.
- **Import Inventory Stock is all or nothing** and only posts opening stock for materials with no movements yet.

## Stubs and gaps left for later

- **Store Purchase Orders and Goods Receipts have no screens.** The API takes a Store location (tested); the PO and GRN screens are Project-only, so stock reaches a store today through transfers or opening-stock import. Making those screens location-generic is the next small change.
- **Stock valuation** (weighted average) is not computed; Received entries carry the GRN rate for M6 / M7.
- **Notifications** (approvals, minimum stock, deliveries) are events only (`document.approved`, `StockBelowMinimum`, `GoodsReceiptPosted`); M9 delivers them.
- **Supplier payments** (M7) will block editing a paid GRN through the `GoodsReceiptPayments` port, which answers "not paid" until then.
- **Not in M5** (CM-0015 §14): RFQ / comparative statements, multi-level approval, unit conversion, ITC / RCM / TDS 194Q, e-way bill generation, Tally sync, material reconciliation per contractor, MR → PR conversion.
- **Cross-context reads.** Some procurement reads of other contexts' tables (suppliers on a location, Team Member names, Company name, material minimums for the watcher, store form options) are direct Prisma reads in procurement infrastructure or `src/composition`, like `PrismaUploaderNames` in projects, rather than through `ProcurementDirectory`. Worth folding behind the port when M7 adds its own.
- **Dev-server only:** in `next dev` the cached `PrismaClient` can come from a different runtime instance than the imported `Prisma` namespace, so `Prisma.DbNull` is written as `{}` (the audit log has had this since M1) and nested `Prisma.sql` fragments in tagged `$queryRaw` templates break. Procurement now uses the app's `$queryRaw(Prisma.sql…)` form; tests and a production build write real NULLs. A separate fix for the db package is suggested.
