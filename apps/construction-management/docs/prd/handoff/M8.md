## Decisions

The sixteen open questions of `modules/05` are answered in [ADR CM-0019](../../adr/CM-0019-tracking-product-decisions.md).

**The owner settled four before the build:**

- **A frozen baseline** with Re-baseline and its history; earned value from completed quantity; Project Progress % weighted by Total Price.
- **Worksheets feed task progress** when the units match.
- **On Issues, Approve verifies and Reject reopens;** on Tasks they gate nothing.
- **A rejected inspection is final;** its page offers Raise re-inspection and the reject dialog can also raise a snag.

Every rule the build settled is in `modules/05` → "Decisions for the build", by ticket. These are the ones most worth a look:

- **Price weighting needs every leaf task priced;** one unpriced task switches the Project to duration weighting, and the gauge says which it used.
- **A task with progress cannot get sub-tasks or switch between quantity and percent;** a task a worksheet names cannot get sub-tasks or be deleted.
- **Status and Delayed are never stored.** They are worked out from the Company's today on every read, so nothing goes stale and no job flips them.
- **Bulk actions on issues and inspections skip and count** what they cannot act on; bulk delete of tasks is all or nothing.
- **An Inspection Date may be a future day** (requests are raised ahead of the visit); an Issue Date may not.
- **Solve, Verify, Reopen, Approve and Reject check no back-dated limits;** they are not entries on the date.
- **Files on tasks and issues reach the Gallery only for members with View all,** because their Read follows View all.
- **Without View all, the dashboard and the Task and Issue & Snag reports count only the member's own and assigned rows;** Project Progress % is always the whole Project's.

## Stubs and gaps left for later

- **Events have no listeners:** `TaskAssigned`, `TaskProgressRecorded`, `TaskCompleted`, `IssueAssigned`, `IssueUpdated`, `IssueSolved`, `IssueReopened`, `InspectionRequested` and `InspectionDecided` are for M9 notifications, as is the "became delayed" push.
- **Not in M8** (CM-0019 §15): predecessors / successors, a working calendar, drag-to-reschedule and Gantt export; BOQ items, Measurement Book, JMR, RA bills and inspections gating completion or bills; inspection checklists; DLP and handover; pinning to a drawing; SLA by priority; Wing-level RERA progress.
- **The Reopen dialog** stops at 500 characters; the server accepts 1,000.
- **Dev-server only:** `next dev` writes untracked `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` into `apps/construction-management`; delete them, never commit them.

## Open questions for the owner

1. Are the CM-0019 defaults right? In particular:
   - Task No. as a plain counter per Project (`T-12`), not a numbering rule in Settings;
   - three levels of tasks at most;
   - the inspector optional, and anyone with Approve / Reject deciding, not only the inspector;
   - the new Quality Control and Structural Engineer designation templates.
2. Should one unpriced task really switch the whole Project to duration weighting, or should unpriced tasks simply be left out of a price-weighted figure?
3. Should Task Approve / Reject gate Mark As Completed (an engineer completes, a manager approves)? They gate nothing today.
4. Should a snag raised from a rejected inspection be assigned to someone by default, for example the inspection's inspector or the Contractor's representative?
5. Worksheets post their quantity only when the unit matches the task's. Is a unit conversion needed (for example sqft on the sheet, sqm on the task), or is "linked only" enough?
