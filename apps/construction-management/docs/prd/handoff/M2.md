## Owner decisions taken during M2

- **Sign-in:** email + password while SMS is off (ADR CM-0009).
- **Monthly wage:** calendar-day proration — wage ÷ days in that month × paid days (Present 1, Half 0.5, Holiday 1, Paid Leave 1).
- **Daily wage:** Holiday and weekly off are unpaid unless the day is marked Paid Leave.

## Spec changes (all written into `modules/08` "Decisions for the build", `modules/02` and the board's Delivered notes)

- Labour and Vendor registers live in the `labour` context (opening balance in the same transaction as the party); Categories, Departments, Supervisors in `masters`; Project in `projects`. Reads across contexts go through ports implemented with plain reads.
- Supervisors are a master (a name, optionally a Team Member); menu `masters.labours`.
- Departments seed list is 53 names (the spec said ~54).
- Opening balance positive = owed to the Labour/Vendor, negative = advance given before the app.
- Hide = soft delete (only without attendance and payments); Inactive drops from attendance.
- One attendance row per Labour per day, in the Project they were on that day; a transfer dated on or before their latest attendance in the old Project is refused.
- OT: > 0 and ≤ 24 hours per line and per day; none on an Absent day; rate defaults to the Labour's OT wage; a re-mark without a rate keeps the saved rate.
- Vendor OT hours are a total per line; half day = 0.5; an edit re-prices from the current rate card.
- Financial hides amounts in responses; entering amounts is allowed (one rule for Labours and Vendors).
- Balances belong to the party (party-wide across Projects); the Project decides who is listed.
- Payments: no edit (cancel and re-enter); a vendor must be assigned to the Project; inactive parties can be paid; future dates refused. Back-dated guard uses the Company defaults and the Financial Closing Date (the catalogue has no payment module key).
- Muster roll covers the Company's own Labours only (vendor heads are not named); deductions are 0 (no deductions engine yet); advances paid in the month are shown and taken off net payable.
