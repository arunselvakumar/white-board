## Decisions

The owner's open questions in `modules/10` were answered with recommended defaults in [ADR CM-0012](../../adr/CM-0012-hrms-product-decisions.md). Every rule the build settled is listed in `modules/10-hrms.md` → "Decisions for the build", grouped by ticket. The ones most worth a look:

- **GPS modes** Disabled / Record only / Required (CM-0012 §1). Record only accepts out-of-fence check-ins into Approvals.
- **Leave year** is a setting (calendar by default, Apr–Mar available). Carry forward runs when the new year is first initialised or accrued.
- **Salary**: calendar-day proration (as for monthly labour wages in M2); overtime paid only when the shift allows it, at 2×; advances recovered automatically in instalments; Mark Paid records the payment but does not post to accounts (Finance is M7); approving a month locks attendance and leave for those members.
- **Statutory**: PF on PF-wage components capped at the ceiling unless the structure turns the cap off; ESI by contribution-period eligibility; PT by state slab (Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, West Bengal seeded; Gujarat and Tamil Nadu not, see CM-0008).

## Stubs and gaps left for later

- **Notifications** for approvals (M9) and an **offline check-in queue** (M12) are not built.
- **Mark Paid does not post to accounts**: M7 should post existing Paid salary slips to the company bank / cash ledger.
- **Map tiles** come from public OpenStreetMap, whose policy discourages production traffic: pick a keyed tile provider before launch.
- **Per-branch holiday lists** (rebuild recommendation 16) and a way to end a shift assignment back to the Settings day are not built.
- The dashboard's past trend days count members active today (the directory has no joined date).
- No recovery schedule view for an advance (the slip shows what was recovered).
- Configuration lists are not paginated (small, bounded lists); leave lists are capped with a total.
- Projects still shows in the sidebar for HRMS Team Members (they are redirected to HRMS).
