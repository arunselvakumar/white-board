## Decisions

The sixteen open questions of `modules/04` are answered in [ADR CM-0016](../../adr/CM-0016-daily-site-work-product-decisions.md).

**The owner settled four before the build:**

- Approve / Reject with a reason;
- two labour counts prefilled from attendance;
- a full site diary with a Hindrance register;
- fuel recorded, not taken from inventory.

Every rule the build settled is in `modules/04` → "Decisions for the build", by ticket. These are the ones most worth a look.

**Equipment and settings:**

- **Equipment lives in site work**, not masters. Its Project, maintenance and availability are checked in the same transaction as each usage sheet.
- **Form settings are per Project.** There is no Company default.

**Approval and stock:**

- **Approval does not gate stock.** Material lines post when a sheet is saved, as in legacy. A rejected sheet keeps its postings until it is edited or deleted. Rejected sheets are left out of reports, the dashboard and the DPR.
- **A worksheet's Location is required only when the Project has a place to pick** (a Wing, Amenity, Common Development or Location). A bare Project can still record work.

**Usage sheets:**

- **"Chargeable"** on rented equipment means its idle hours are billed.
- **Monthly hire** is billed per day at the monthly rate ÷ days in that month.

**Progress Reports:**

- **The DPR uses its own Progress Report menu**, not the Reports page.
- Downloading a DPR that shows hire cost needs Financial on Equipment Usage.

## Stubs and gaps left for later

- **Events have no listeners:**
  - `EquipmentHireAccrued` is for M7 hire bills and TDS 194I.
  - `EquipmentFuelOverrun` and `document.approved` are for M9 notifications.
  - Maintenance cost does not reach expenses.
- **No Task link.** Worksheets have no Task, and work done does not roll up into progress; M8 adds both. The Task Progress Report waits for M8.
- **Not in M6** (CM-0016 §17):
  - per-line or multi-level approval;
  - material reconciliation per contractor;
  - counts per Labour Category;
  - fuel from inventory;
  - maintenance schedules;
  - offline entry, WhatsApp submission, geo-tagged or drawing-pinned photos;
  - worksheet backups.
- **Dev-server only:** `next dev` writes `Prisma.DbNull` as `{}`, the M5 note. Site-work stores now write a real NULL for "no location", and every reader goes through `storedLocationRef`. Other contexts' JSON columns still have the issue.
- **New dependency:** `sharp` turns WebP thumbnails into images the PDF writer can embed.

DATABASE_URL=postgresql://whiteboard:whiteboard@localhost:5433/construction bun run --filter @repo/construction-db migrate:deploy

Restart the dev server and run `bun install` after pulling: there are new Prisma models and `sharp`.

## Open questions for the owner

1. Are the CM-0016 defaults right? In particular:
   - Equipment living in site work;
   - per-Project form settings;
   - approval not gating stock;
   - "Chargeable" meaning billed idle hours.
2. Should a rejected worksheet's material postings be reversed automatically, instead of staying until the sheet is edited or deleted?
3. Is the site-diary weather list (Sunny, Cloudy, Light rain, Heavy rain, Windy, Foggy, Very hot) enough, or do you want temperature or working hours lost to weather on the diary itself?
4. Should hindrance sign-off become a real two-party approval (both sides confirm in the app), or is recording the paper sign-off enough?
5. The DPR keeps PDFs until someone deletes them. Do you want an automatic expiry?
