## 2. Indian compliance the product must model

### GST

- **Works contract (SAC 9954)**: default 18% with ITC. The 56th GST Council (3 Sep 2025, effective 22 Sep 2025) moved several formerly-12% categories (government earthwork >75%, offshore oil/gas, linked subcontracts) to 18% ([EY](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-in/alerts-hub/2025/09/navigating-gst-changes-in-the-real-estate-and-construction-ecosystem.pdf)). Labour-only contracts are sometimes classified under 9987 — classification must be per work-order, not global ([TaxBuddy](https://www.taxbuddy.com/blog/gst-9954-hsn-code-works-contract-impact)).
- **Residential real estate (promoter)**: 1% affordable (≤60 sq m metro / 90 sq m non-metro, ≤₹45L) and 5% other, both without ITC, for projects from 1 Apr 2019. Conditions: ≥80% of inputs/input services from registered suppliers computed per project per FY; shortfall taxed under RCM at 18%; cement from unregistered suppliers always under RCM at the cement rate ([Taxmann](https://taxmann.com/post/blog/gst-on-real-estate), [TaxGuru 80/20](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/practical-gst-point-builders-developers-overlook-80-20-rule-paying.html)). Model: per-project registered/unregistered purchase ledger, FY-end shortfall computation.
- **Cement**: 28% → 18% from 22 Sep 2025; sand-lime bricks, granite/marble blocks 12% → 5% ([NBM&CW](https://www.nbmcw.com/news/gst-council-slashes-cement-tax-to-18-cuts-rates-on-key-construction-materials.html)). HSN rate tables must be effective-dated.
- **ITC**: Section 17(5) blocks ITC on construction of immovable property on own account (owner/developer side); contractors keep ITC when supplying works contract services onward ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-on-construction)). The system needs an "ITC eligible?" flag per purchase derived from project type.
- **E-invoicing**: mandatory for B2B/SEZ/export invoices when AATO > ₹5 crore in any FY since 2017-18 (Notification 10/2023, from 1 Aug 2023); AATO ≥ ₹10 crore must report to IRP within 30 days of invoice date (from 1 Apr 2025) ([echai](https://echai.ventures/d2c/d2c-gst-multistate/e-invoicing-e-way-bill-thresholds)). Applies to RA bills, other-party sales invoices, scrap sales.
- **E-way bill**: consignment value > ₹50,000 regardless of turnover; since 1 Mar 2024 no e-way bill without e-invoice details where e-invoicing applies ([Fox Mandal](https://foxmandal.in/News/e-way-bill-without-e-invoice-details-not-allowed-from-march-1/)). Material transfer between sites and central-store delivery notes should carry a delivery-challan + e-way-bill reference.

### RERA

- Promoter must register any project > 500 sq m or > 8 units before marketing; disclosures include sanctioned plans, timelines, and the designated bank account ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/importance-rera-compliances-real-estate-project-registration.html)).
- **70% rule** (s.4(2)(l)(D)): 70% of allottee receipts into a separate scheduled-bank account; withdrawals in proportion to percentage completion, certified by architect (Form 1), engineer (Form 2) and CA (Form 3); annual audit within 6 months of FY end ([TaxGuru withdrawal](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/rera-financial-management-discipline-withdrawal-funds-rera.html), [WIRC](https://old.wirc-icai.org/images/material/Rera-Compliances-Builders-Parikshit.pdf)).
- **Quarterly progress reports**: state-prescribed; MahaRERA issued show-cause notices to 8,212 projects for the Jan–Mar 2026 QPR and gave 60 days before penalties, account freezes and sales curbs ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/maharera-flags-8212-projects-for-missing-qpr-deadline-gives-regulatory-warning)). Penalty up to 5% of estimated project cost ([Terra Insight](https://www.terra-insight.com/insights/rera-compliance-quarterly-form-progress-report-real-estate-india)). Form numbering and deadlines (7/15/20 days after quarter) differ by state and year — make them configurable per state RERA.
- 5-year structural defect liability to allottees, separate from contractual DLP ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/articles/lacunae-in-indian-law-regarding-latent-defects-in-construction-contracts-253439)).
- Product implication: wing/floor/unit model must produce physical % complete per building/wing (architect certificate), cost incurred (land vs construction) per quarter, allottee receipts vs. 70% account ledger, and inventory status (sold/unsold) — the inputs for Forms 1–3 and the QPR.

### Labour and welfare

- **BOCW Act 1996 + Cess Act**: establishments with ≥10 (CAG says "more than 10") building workers in preceding 12 months register within 60 days; **cess 1% of cost of construction** (excluding land and employer BOCW compensation), excluding residences costing ≤ ₹10 lakh; registered workers get benefit cards ([factoHR](https://factohr.com/?p=48887), [Lakshmikumaran](https://lakshmisri.com/newsroom/archives/labour-welfare-cess-and-its-implications-for-the-construction-industry)).
- **ESI**: 10+ employees (20 in some states), wage ceiling ₹21,000 (₹25,000 PwD); employer 3.25%, employee 0.75% ([calcguru](https://calcguru.in/esi-applicability-wage-limit-contribution-rates/)).
- **PF**: 20+ employees; wage ceiling ₹15,000 (₹21,000 proposed, not notified); 12% + 12%; Social Security Code extends applicability to all establishments ([calcguru](https://calcguru.in/?p=4238)).
- **Labour Codes 2020**: all four Codes in force 21 Nov 2025; central rules notified 8–9 May 2026 (apply where Centre is appropriate government — PSUs, mines, ports and their contractors); states still finalising rules ([Trilegal](https://trilegal.com/knowledge-repository/trilegal-update-labour-codes-move-closer-to-full-implementation-takeaways-from-the-newly-notified-central-rules/), [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/legal-update-labour-codes-move-from-framework-to-function-central-rules-notified/1791166)). OSH rules: contractor wage timelines, principal-employer liability on contractor default, single multi-state contractor licence, electronic wage slips and records, grievance mechanism for contract labour.
- **Registers**: legacy CLRA Rule 78 — Form XVI muster roll, Form XVII wage register, Form XVIII combined when wage period ≤ fortnight, Form XIX wage slip, XX–XXIII deductions/fines/advances/overtime ([greytHR](https://community.greythr.com/t/the-contract-labour-regulation-abolition-act-1970/34)). Ease of Compliance Rules 2017 collapse 56 registers to 5 combined registers ([Mondaq](https://mondaq.com/india/employee-rights-labour-relations/575584/reformation-of-the-labour-laws-compliance-rules)); the Labour Ministry handbook calls it an "attendance register cum muster roll" ([Kredily](https://kredily.com/attendance-register/)). Minimum wages are state-notified per skill category (unskilled/semi/skilled/highly skilled) and revised twice a year via VDA — store as dated rate cards per state.
- Product implication: labour attendance must emit Form XVI/XVII (or combined) and ESI/PF challan inputs per contractor; party master needs BOCW registration, CLRA licence, PF/ESI codes.

## 3. Domain practices to model

- **BOQ and rate analysis**: CPWD DSR (2023 civil; 2025 E&M) items with IS 1200 measurement rules; rates built from material + wastage, labour output norms × wage, plant, then overheads/profit (CPWD uses 15% contractor profit & overhead on analysed rates) ([infralens DSR](https://infralens.in/knowledge/dsr-2023-complete-guide-civil-em-walkthrough), [constructionestimatorindia](https://constructionestimatorindia.com/?p=16805)). Non-schedule items need market rate analysis. Model: item library keyed to DSR code, project BOQ with item rate, quantity, unit; rate analysis sheet per item with resource coefficients (the same coefficients drive material reconciliation).
- **Measurement Book (MB) and RA bills**: measurements recorded by length × breadth × depth per location against BOQ item, abstract per bill; RA bill = cumulative measured value − previous bills − deductions (retention, mobilisation recovery, TDS, GST TDS where applicable, material issued at cost, penalties). **JMR** = same sheet signed jointly by contractor and client engineer. **Deductions register** tracks each RA bill's recoveries ([infralens deductions](https://www.infralens.in/pmc/billing/deductions-register-ra-bills)).
- **Retention money**: typically 5–10% of each bill, capped (CPWD: 5% of contract value via security deduction at 10% of each running bill until cap), released 50% at completion and 50% at DLP end, or against bank guarantee ([BHEL tender](https://tenders.bhel.com/sites/default/files/20Highlights-2025-12-06-09%3A57%3A01.pdf)).
- **Mobilisation advance**: typically 10% of contract value against BG, interest-bearing or not, recovered pro-rata from RA bills (e.g. 8.33% or 10% of each bill until recovered) ([infralens](https://infralens.in/pmc/mobilization/advance-payment-application)). Secured/material advances similar.
- **Work orders with item rates**: subcontractor WO = BOQ subset with rates, quantities, validity, terms (retention %, advance, DLP); subcon RA bills measured against WO; variations/extra items via rate analysis.
- **Material reconciliation**: theoretical consumption = executed BOQ quantities × coefficients (e.g. cement 350–400 kg/m³ for M25–M30, steel 80–250 kg/m³ by element, 3% steel wastage norm) vs. actual issues from stores; variance beyond permissible wastage (contract-specified, often 2–5%) recovered at penal rate ([infralens recon](https://infralens.in/pmc/billing/material-reconciliation-statement), [NIT Meghalaya conditions](https://nitm.ac.in/nitmeghalaya/ckfinder/userfiles/files/1_Conditions.pdf)). Needs per-contractor material-issue ledger and reconciliation at each RA bill.
- **Concrete testing (IS 456:2000 cl. 15–16)**: one sample = 3 cubes (150 mm); minimum samples 1 for 1–5 m³, 2 for 6–15, 3 for 16–30, 4 for 31–50, +1 per additional 50 m³; 7-day/28-day tests per IS 516; acceptance: individual ≥ fck − 3 N/mm², mean of 3 consecutive ≥ fck + 0.825σ or fck + 4 ([infralens acceptance](https://infralens.in/handbook/cube-test-acceptance)). Other site tests: slump (IS 1199), brick compressive (IS 3495), steel tensile (IS 1786), sieve analysis (IS 2386), MTC per batch. Model: pour register → cube register → lab result → acceptance flag.
- **DPR / site diary / hindrance register**: site diary is a serially numbered record in the PM's custody of weather, manpower by trade, plant, materials received/consumed, work done by location, visitors, instructions ([IGNOU unit](https://www.egyankosh.ac.in/bitstream/123456789/28816/1/Unit-5.pdf)). Hindrance register records each stoppage (date, cause, affected activity, duration, responsible party), jointly signed, and is the evidence base for extension-of-time and 10CC claims.
- **Escalation (CPWD clause 10CC; 10CA for cement/steel/bitumen price variation)**: index-based formula weighted by labour/material components over the contract period; not applicable where stipulated completion ≤ 18 months under Works Manual 2003 ([CAG](https://saiindia.gov.in/uploads/old_reports/union/union_compliance/2007_2008/Civil/Report_no_2/chap_10.pdf), [infralens escalation](https://infralens.in/pmc/billing/price-escalation-calculation)). Model: escalation bill as a special RA bill type with base and current indices.
- **Defect liability period**: 12 months typical in CPWD/PWD and private contracts, retention/performance security held until end; RERA 5-year structural warranty applies to allottees separately ([infralens DLP](https://www.infralens.in/term/defect-liability-period)). Snag/issue module needs a DLP phase and a handover date per unit.

## 4. Gaps in BuildControl and candidate differentiators

BuildControl already covers breadth (procurement chain, store, HRMS, booking CRM, Gantt, testing). What it lacks relative to 2026 competitors:

1. **WhatsApp as the field UI** — Onsite and Tally both sell "replace WhatsApp" and "send via WhatsApp" ([Onsite](https://onsiteteams.com/still-relying-on-whatsapp-for-construction-dpr-switch-to-onsite/), [Tally WhatsApp](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tallyprime-with-whatsapp-price-list/)). Nobody lets a supervisor _submit_ attendance, DPR photos, or an MR from a WhatsApp Business bot. Highest-leverage differentiator for the labour-contractor tier.
2. **Offline-first mobile** — repeatedly listed as a must for basements/remote sites with auto-sync ([constructionestimatorindia](https://constructionestimatorindia.com/?p=12099)); Powerplay's offline support is unconfirmed; Onsite's complaints include forced updates. Pairs with the "too many steps per entry" complaint: fewer taps, prefilled from yesterday.
3. **Tally / Zoho Books sync as standard, not a ₹25,000 add-on** — Onsite charges ₹20,000 + ₹5,000/yr; Powerplay has none. Push vouchers (purchase, payment, journal for TDS/GST), pull ledgers and party balances.
4. **India statutory outputs** — none of the site apps produce Form XVI/XVII, BOCW cess computation, 194C/194Q TDS ledgers, RERA Form 1–3 inputs, or the 80/20 registered-supplier test. Owning this is defensible because it needs the project/unit/payment model the app already has.
5. **AI photo progress / 360 / drone** — OpenSpace-style progress by trade on floor plans; Indian specialists (Track3D, SitePace, Contineu) sell it standalone at enterprise prices. A cheaper version: geo-tagged, drawing-pinned photo updates per unit/location with AI tagging of activity and quantity suggestion, feeding the DPR and RERA % complete.
6. **Client (allottee) portal + UPI collections** — Sell.Do has the portal and demand letters but no native UPI; payment aggregators settle UPI at 0% MDR ([Innoviti](https://innoviti.com/blogs/how-to-accept-card-upi-and-emi-payments-as-a-real-estate-agents-and-brokers-in-india/)). Demand letter → UPI link → auto-receipt → RERA 70% account ledger is one straight line.
7. **Vendor/subcontractor portal** — self-serve RA bill submission against WO with MB attachment, status of PO/GRN/payments, TDS certificates; only Onsite Enterprise offers it.
8. **Drawing-pinned work** — Powerplay reviewers asked to "tap a drawing to update work done"; BuildControl has drawings and locations but not the link.
9. **Clean public pricing and no lock-in** — both Onsite's Play Store reviews and Powerplay's listing reflect pricing opacity and upgrade pressure. Publish prices; export everything.

## 5. Prioritisation

**Must have for parity (ship first; BuildControl's existing scope, rebuilt)**

- Projects → wings/floors/units, locations; tasks with Gantt; drawings; issues/snags; inspection requests; gallery; progress reports
- Masters: labour, contractors, suppliers, vendors, equipment; numbering sequences; roles/menu permissions; back-dated entry control; subscriptions
- Procurement chain: MR → PR → RFQ → PO → GRN → inventory → material transfer/delivery challan; central store; petty cash; party payments; other-party sales invoices
- Labour/vendor attendance with wages and advances; equipment usage with fuel
- Daily worksheet/DPR with photos; testing reports
- Booking/inquiry CRM funnel; HRMS (geo-fence attendance, leaves, shifts, holidays, salary structures)

**Must have for India (what competitors leave to Tally or the CA)**

- GST: effective-dated HSN/SAC rate table, per-work-order classification, ITC-eligible flag, 80/20 registered-supplier test and RCM for promoters, e-invoice (IRN) and e-way bill generation on sales invoices and material transfers
- TDS: party PAN/entity type, 194C/194J/194Q/194I with per-FY threshold tracking, TDS ledger, 26Q export
- BOQ with DSR item library, rate analysis, work orders at item rates, MB/JMR, RA bills with retention, mobilisation recovery, deductions register, escalation bill, DLP release
- Material reconciliation per contractor per RA bill
