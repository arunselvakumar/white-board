# Cool-tinted brand palette around #595FAE

Whiteboard’s primary is `#595FAE` (v1 `primary-600`, v2 `brand-500`). v2 pairs it with warm paper (`#FAFAF7`). We are not copying that canvas.

Surfaces are near-white with a violet tint; ink leans blue-violet; a 50–950 brand scale sits on that primary. Dark mode brightens primary one step. We picked this over warm paper (too “school”) and over stock shadcn neutrals (no brand in the chrome).
